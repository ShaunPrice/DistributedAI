Setting up your own installation?

The installer creates your first organisation and administrator credential. Open .secrets/bootstrap_token in the folder where you installed DistributedAI, then paste its contents above. This is your administrator access token, not a project code.

After signing in, create a project, add people or AI clients, and assign their access. If you are joining someone else's installation, ask its administrator for your own token.

ORGANISATION SECURITY

Encryption keys

Your content uses AES-256-GCM. Choose an approved key provider or supply a new key.

Leave the key blank to generate it securely. Supplied keys are never displayed again. Rotation keeps older content readable using its earlier key version.

Keys are not exposed in the platform console.

Cloud key providers are configured by your deployment operator. Protecting content from infrastructure operators requires additional isolation, such as client-side encryption.
